Customer help

Availability requests

Choose an LED pod quantity and send a free request. Stock and delivery dates are pending confirmation. A request records your preference without a reservation, payment or delivery commitment.

For availability or fitment questions, contact us on WhatsApp.

Help with a previous order

Use the order reference shown at checkout and the same mobile number to check your order. For changes, cancellations, damaged items or return questions, contact Beampak on WhatsApp with your order reference.

How we use your data

Availability requests record the selected product and quantity, the price shown, and campaign labels when present. Your browser stores a random identifier so repeat requests for the same product update the quantity. The request form does not ask for a name, phone number or address.

We keep previous order details available to authorised store staff for order support. Customers can look up an earlier order with its reference and checkout mobile number. Messages you choose to send on WhatsApp are handled through WhatsApp.
