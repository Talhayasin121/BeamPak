Customer help

Delivery and payment

Beampak takes orders for delivery in Pakistan. Pay in Pakistani rupees on delivery. Delivery is Rs. 249 for orders below Rs. 5,000 and free from Rs. 5,000. Review the full amount in your bag before placing an order.

Your order starts as Received. For an estimated delivery date or fitment advice, contact us on WhatsApp.

Tracking and order changes

Use the order reference shown at checkout and the same mobile number to check your order. For changes, cancellations, damaged items or return questions, contact Beampak on WhatsApp with your order reference.

Contact Beampak on WhatsApp Track an order

How we use your data

Checkout collects your name, mobile number, city, delivery address and items ordered so Beampak can process, deliver and support your order. The order manager gives authorised store staff access to these details. Customers can look up order items, total and status using their order reference and mobile number.

Your browser stores the items in your bag so you can come back to it. A checkout attempt identifier and a hash of the submitted details help the site recover an order if the connection drops. Use WhatsApp for requests about your order data. Messages you send there are handled through WhatsApp.
