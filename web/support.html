Customer help

Checkout and delivery

Choose a quantity and review your selection at checkout. Stock and delivery are pending, and no payment is due.

For fitment questions, contact us on WhatsApp.

Help with a previous order

Use the order reference shown at checkout and the same mobile number to check your order. For changes, cancellations, damaged items or return questions, contact Beampak on WhatsApp with your order reference.

How we use your data

When you confirm at checkout, we save your name, street address, email address, optional mobile number, selected item, quantity, price and campaign labels when present. We use these details to understand demand and prevent repeat submissions. Your browser stores a random identifier for this purpose. Authorised Beampak staff can view the details.

We keep previous order details available to authorised store staff for order support. Customers can look up an earlier order with its reference and checkout mobile number. Messages you choose to send on WhatsApp are handled through WhatsApp.
