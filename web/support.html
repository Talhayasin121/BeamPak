How we use your data

When you confirm at checkout, we save your name, street address, email address, optional mobile number, selected item, quantity, optional extra remote, price and campaign labels when present. We use these details to understand demand and prevent repeat submissions. Your browser stores a random identifier for this purpose. Authorised Beampak staff can view the details.

To understand how our shop is used, we record page visits and shopping actions with random browser and visit identifiers. Activity includes the pages viewed, bag changes, checkout steps, visible page time, device category and campaign labels. It is visible to authorised Beampak staff. Form details are saved when you confirm at checkout.

We keep previous order details available to authorised store staff for order support. Customers can look up an earlier order with its reference and checkout mobile number. Messages you choose to send on WhatsApp are handled through WhatsApp.

Advertising preferences

When Meta Pixel is configured, it automatically reports page visits, product views, additions to your bag, checkout starts, saved confirmations and WhatsApp clicks to Meta. Meta may use browser and device information and cookies to measure ads.

Confirmations are measured as leads. Details entered in the checkout form are kept in Beampak’s database. You can stop ad measurement using the button below. Previous opt-out choices remain respected.
