PRODUCT FAQs.

What does one piece mean, and what does it cost?

One piece is one LED pod at Rs. 999. Two pods cost Rs. 1,998, three cost Rs. 2,997 and four cost Rs. 3,996. An optional extra remote adds Rs. 100 to the selection subtotal.

Can I choose more than four pods?

Yes. Use the quantity buttons or enter a whole number from 1 to 20. The bundle picture, included accessories and subtotal update with your selection.

What is included with my chosen quantity?

Every bundle includes your chosen number of LED pods and one remote. Each pod comes with one mounting pad and one rubber ring. A single-pod bundle includes one single charging lead. Larger bundles include one split lead per pair of pods, plus a single lead for an odd remaining pod.

Can I add an extra remote?

Yes. Tick “Add an extra remote” to include one additional remote for Rs. 100. Your bundle already includes one remote, so selecting the option gives you two remotes in total. The Rs. 100 charge applies once to the bundle.

What colours and effects are available?

Choose white, red, green, blue, ice blue, yellow, pink or purple. The remote selects steady, slow-flash and fast-flash effects.

How do I charge a pod?

Connect the included compatible USB Type-C lead to the pod and a suitable USB source. Follow the charging input marked on the unit. Charge in a dry place, then unplug and close the port cover if fitted.

How long does a full charge take?

A full charge takes approximately one hour. The indicator is red while charging and switches off when full. Charging time varies with the power source, cable and battery condition.

What are the battery capacity and voltage?

The built-in rechargeable battery has a 110mAh capacity and a 3.7V voltage rating.

How far does the remote work?

The remote works at approximately seven metres. Test reception at your chosen mounting point before securing the pods.

Can one remote control several pods?

One remote is included for the pods in your bundle. Use its power, colour and mode buttons to control the lights, following the kit’s operating instructions.

Will it fit a Honda Civic, Toyota Grande or bike?

These surface-mounted accent lights can be fitted where there is a secure mounting surface and enough clearance. Keep them away from pedals, steering parts, seat rails and airbag panels. On a bike, keep clear of the exhaust, wheels, chain and suspension.

How do I mount the pods?

Test each light while parked. Clean and dry the mounting surface, then secure the pod with a compatible mounting pad or use the rubber ring around a suitable bar or frame section. Check that the light cannot move or interfere with the vehicle.

Are the pods waterproof?

The pods have an IP67 dust and water protection rating. Close the charging-port cover if fitted and make sure the pod and connection are dry before charging. Follow the unit’s instructions for water exposure and cleaning.

Can these replace headlights, brake lights or indicators?

These pods provide decorative accent lighting for car interiors and parked motorcycle displays. Keep all factory lights and signals operational. Check current local rules before using coloured or flashing lights on public roads.

What should I try if the light or remote does not respond?

Charge the pod, check its power button and check the remote battery. Remove a battery-isolation tab if fitted, as directed by the kit instructions. Try the remote close to one pod and follow any pairing steps. Contact Beampak on WhatsApp if the issue continues.

How should I clean and store a pod?

Switch it off and disconnect the charging lead. Clean the exterior with a soft, lightly damp cloth, then dry it fully. Keep moisture away from the charging connection and store the pod away from excessive heat.

What does the one-year warranty cover?

The one-year warranty covers mechanical defects. For assistance, send Beampak your order reference and a description of the issue on WhatsApp at 03309153823.

What is the housing made of, and where is it made?

The pod has a black plastic housing and is made in China. The product itself is unbranded.

Can I request a custom design or a sample?

Design customisation, samples and minor adjustments are available to enquire about for custom or bulk orders. Send your requirements to Beampak on WhatsApp for a quote before placing a custom order.

What quality checks are used?

The production process includes a pre-production sample and a final inspection before shipment.

What happens after I confirm my selection?

The product is currently unavailable. Confirming saves your contact details, chosen quantity and extra-remote selection, then shows the unavailable message. No payment is collected and no shipment is arranged. Your selection is saved, so there is no need to submit it again.

Can I contact Beampak at any time?

You can send a WhatsApp message to 03309153823 at any time, 24 hours a day. Messages are answered as soon as possible. Include the email address used at checkout when asking about a saved selection.
