PRODUCT FAQs.

How do I charge the pods?

Use a compatible USB charging cable to connect the pods to a USB power source that matches the input printed on the kit or in its instructions. Follow the kit’s charging indicator guidance if provided.

How long does a full charge take, and how long will it last?

Charging time and runtime for this exact kit are unverified. Runtime can vary with brightness, lighting mode and battery condition. Battery service life and charge-cycle ratings are also unverified.

Do I need to splice into the vehicle wiring?

The pods are rechargeable and are designed to be used independently of the vehicle wiring. Use a compatible USB cable to recharge them.

Will the pods fit a Civic, Grande or motorcycle?

These are surface-mounted accent lights. Fit depends on the space and mounting surface available. Check that the pods can sit securely and stay clear of vehicle controls and moving parts.

How should I install them?

Test the light and remote while parked. Clean and dry the mounting surface, then use a compatible mounting pad or ring where it can hold the pod securely. Check for movement before use. Keep the remote and cable clear of driving controls.

Can I use the lights in rain or while driving?

Keep the pods dry until their water resistance is verified. Use coloured or flashing effects for parked displays, and keep all factory lights and signals clear.

What if a pod or the remote doesn’t respond?

Check that the pods are charged and switched on. Try the remote close to the pod, and follow any activation or pairing steps in the kit’s instructions.
